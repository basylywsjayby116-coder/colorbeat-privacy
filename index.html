Color Beat - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Color Beat ("the app"), developed by Basilious Agaeby ("I", "me"), handles information. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information I collect

I do not collect, store, or share any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location. The app has no accounts and no sign-in.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your best scores and your music on/off setting locally on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads using Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). AdMob may collect and use certain information automatically, such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data, in order to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. This information is handled by Google, not by me.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). To learn more about how Google uses data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
